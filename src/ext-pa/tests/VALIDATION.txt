Final PA command policy validation — 2026-11-15

root-slack gain. Timing is always enabled: bounded RW/RF and strict RS.
Native operator controls and explicit -c mapped clustering remain supported.
Alternative score/weight/effort selectors, full-cone scoring and the extra
balanced-refactor candidate were removed from the active implementation.
